Context:
Review the current currency gain/loss calculation logic. 
I need to implement the business logic to calculate Total Gain/Loss, Capital Gain (Stock Performance), and Currency Gain/Loss (FX Impact).

The Problem:Users buy stocks in foreign currencies (e.g., USD) using a base currency (e.g., AUD). When the exchange rate fluctuates, it affects their total return in their base currency. 
I need to isolate how much gain/loss comes from the stock price movement vs. the currency fluctuation.

Variables:
 - Qty: Quantity of shares held.
 - Price_Buy: Unit price of the stock at purchase (in Foreign Currency).
 - Price_Current: Current unit price of the stock (in Foreign Currency).
 - FX_Buy: Exchange rate at the time of purchase (Base / Foreign).
 - FX_Current: Current exchange rate (Base / Foreign).
 
 Required Logic & Formulas:
 Please implement the following logic. Note that "Foreign Value" refers to Price * Qty.
 
1. Total Gain (Base Currency):
The simple difference between what the position is worth now (in Base) versus what was paid (in Base).
   $$TotalGain = (Price_{Current} \times Qty \times FX_{Current}) - (Price_{Buy} \times Qty \times FX_{Buy})$$
   
2. Capital Gain (Stock Component): Calculates the gain driven only by stock price movement, neutralizing FX by using the original purchase rate.
  $$CapitalGain = (Price_{Current} - Price_{Buy}) \times Qty \times FX_{Buy}$$
  
3.FX Gain (Currency Component):Calculates the impact of the exchange rate fluctuation on the current total value of the asset.

  $$FXGain = (Price_{Current} \times Qty) \times (FX_{Current} - FX_{Buy})$$
  
  (Note: Ensure that Capital Gain + FX Gain roughly equals Total Gain)
